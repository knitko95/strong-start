Rose athlete character draft

The female body, game-engine rig and base skin texture are MakeHuman graphical assets, CC0. The model is adapted with independently authored fitted athletic colors, a mobile surface reduction, pupils, trainers and laces. The supplied fitness mockup informs the visual direction; its pixels are not used as model textures.

Skin source: MakeHuman young light-skinned female diffuse texture. Official system asset and CC0 provenance. Mesh/rig: MPFB source revision. Graphical asset license.

The character uses the existing 55-bone runtime skeleton, inverse binds, animation and equipment without retargeting. The anatomical instructor remains separately available; its fitted muscle maps are not applied to this clothed character. Anatomical instructor, animation and renderer credits.

The current revision includes generated UV facial-color, iris and strand textures, additional head geometry, an adapted authored bun with fine alpha-tested flyaways, surface roughness differences and a rounded supported bust under the athletic top. The source prompts and generation method are recorded in the public texture record. The existing 55-bone rig and motion remain unchanged.

The main hair adapts Rehman Polanski / RehmanPolanski’s rehmanpolanski_hair_bun_brown mesh and texture, explicitly listed as CC0 in the official Hair 01 register. The original files, legacy exporter headers and adaptation record are retained in the source provenance. The bun was fitted and reshaped in Blender; added loose wisps reuse the generated strand source.

This model is a visual draft. Physical-phone performance, exercise contacts and professional technique review remain unverified.
