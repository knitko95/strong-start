Rose athlete character draft

The female body, game-engine rig and base skin texture are MakeHuman graphical assets, CC0. The model is adapted with independently authored fitted athletic colors, a mobile surface reduction, swept hair strands and bun, pupils, trainers and laces. The supplied fitness mockup informs the visual direction; its pixels are not used as model textures.

Skin source: MakeHuman young light-skinned female diffuse texture. Official system asset and CC0 provenance. Mesh/rig: MPFB source revision. Graphical asset license.

The character uses the existing 55-bone runtime skeleton, inverse binds, animation and equipment without retargeting. The anatomical instructor remains separately available; its fitted muscle maps are not applied to this clothed character. Anatomical instructor, animation and renderer credits.

The current revision includes generated UV facial-color, iris and strand textures, additional head geometry, layered alpha-tested hair, surface roughness differences and a larger covered bust. The source prompts and generation method are recorded in the public texture record. The existing 55-bone rig and motion remain unchanged.

This model is a visual draft. Physical-phone performance, exercise contacts and professional technique review remain unverified.
