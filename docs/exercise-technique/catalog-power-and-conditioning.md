## Kettlebell Swing

Reference relationship: **same-movement**. Status: internal research draft.

**Setup.** Use the two-hand chest-height version, setting the bell slightly ahead and a grip that permits a controlled hike.

**How to perform.** Drive the hips briskly to float the bell, then hinge as it returns and park it deliberately after the final rep.

**Alignment and range.** Arms guide the bell; the hip action supplies the drive. Finish tall without leaning backward and keep the bell close during the backswing.

**Breathing and pace.** Exhale briefly on the drive and inhale as the bell returns. Maintain a repeatable rhythm rather than a slow arm raise.

**Common mistakes.** Squatting excessively, lifting with the shoulders, rounding at the bottom or hyperextending the back at the top.

**Easier version.** Practise a kettlebell deadlift and hike-to-park before continuous swings.

**Variation details and limits.** Do not turn this into an overhead swing or slow shoulder raise; bell height follows hip power rather than arm pulling.

**When to stop or regress.** End or regress when the described alignment, support or breathing is lost. Stop for sharp pain, pinching, numbness or tingling; individual symptoms need appropriate professional assessment.

**References reviewed.** [PureGym: Two Handed Kettlebell Swings](https://www.puregym.com/exercises/plyometrics/kettlebells/two-hand-kb-swing/) ([review limits](sources.md#p202)).

Movement relationship describes reference relevance, not professional validation. Notes are original synthesis; differing equipment/support, progressions, breathing and conservative range choices may be adaptations. See the variant notes and each source's limits.

<a id="skater-bound"></a>
## Skater Bound

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

**Setup.** Start balanced on one leg with clear space for a modest sideways bound.

**How to perform.** Push off the support leg, travel laterally to the other foot and hold the landing briefly before returning.

**Alignment and range.** Keep the knees tracking with the feet. Choose height and distance that allow a quiet balanced landing without a deep emergency tuck.

**Breathing and pace.** Breathe naturally; take a fresh setup between discrete efforts. Stop a set when landing quality or jump output deteriorates.

**Common mistakes.** Stiff-legged landings, knee collapse, chasing box height through excessive knee tuck or rushing fatigued repeats.

**Easier version.** Use a lower jump, smaller distance or a step-up and practise landing on the floor.

**Variation details and limits.** A stable landing matters more than distance; the trailing leg should not whip the pelvis into rotation.

**When to stop or regress.** End or regress when the described alignment, support or breathing is lost. Stop for sharp pain, pinching, numbness or tingling; individual symptoms need appropriate professional assessment.

## Battle Rope Alternating Waves

Reference relationship: **same-movement**. Status: internal research draft.

**Setup.** Hold one rope end per hand with slack in front and soft hips/knees.

**How to perform.** Move the hands alternately up and down to send repeated waves toward the anchor.

**Alignment and range.** Let hips and feet contribute when the variation calls for it; avoid repeated uncontrolled lumbar flexion or twisting against fixed knees.

**Breathing and pace.** Keep breathing rhythmically throughout the interval; reduce pace before position deteriorates.

**Common mistakes.** Gripping until the wrists buckle, rigid knees, uncontrolled back motion or continuing when the waves can no longer be repeated.

**Easier version.** Shorten the interval or wave size and use a slower stationary stance.

**Variation details and limits.** Keep shoulders and wrists comfortable; the moving interval should not become a rigid breath-held isometric grip.

**When to stop or regress.** End or regress when the described alignment, support or breathing is lost. Stop for sharp pain, pinching, numbness or tingling; individual symptoms need appropriate professional assessment.

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

**Setup.** Demonstrate a controlled one-hand swing and stable overhead hold with a manageable bell and clear space.

**How to perform.** Drive from the hips, guide the bell close and insert the hand smoothly into the handle as the arm reaches overhead; lower using a practised close path.
