## Walking and treadmill walking

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

App labels: `Easy treadmill walk`, `Incline walk, picking up the pace`, `Brisk incline walk`, `Steady incline walk`, `Brisk walk outside`, `Incline walk with pushes`, `Incline sprint walks`, `Fast walk intervals outside`, `Easy walk`, `Treadmill walk`, `incline treadmill walk`, `brisk walk outside or around the block`, `Brisk walk`, `Fast incline walk`, `Walk`.

**Setup.** Stand tall with feet supported, familiarise yourself with the start/stop controls and attach a treadmill's emergency cord before starting. Use the actual machine's mounting procedure.

**How to perform.** Walk with a natural arm swing and comfortable stride. Adjust speed or incline gradually while keeping the body over the feet rather than hanging from the rails.

**Alignment and range.** Reduce speed or grade if continuous handrail support, overstriding or backward lean is needed. Slow the belt to a stop before stepping off; do not jump onto the side rails to rest.

**Breathing and pace.** Breathe continuously and reduce the effort before technique breaks down. Pace labels do not change the movement mechanics or establish a suitable intensity for a particular person.

**Easier version.** Use an easier pace, shorter duration, lower resistance or stable low-impact option while keeping the movement controlled.

**References reviewed.** [Life Fitness: Atmos Treadmill Manual Workout and Emergency Stop](https://support.lifefitness.com/hc/en-us/articles/42814171096215-Life-Fitness-Atmos-Treadmill-How-to-Set-Up-a-Manual-Workout-on-Your-SL-Console) ([review limits](sources.md#xtread)); [NHS: Strength exercises](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/strength-exercises/) ([review limits](sources.md#xnhsstrength)).

## Marching and high knees

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

App labels: `March in place`, `Brisk march with arm swings`, `Brisk walk or march in place`, `High knees`.

**Setup.** Use a clear nonslip space and a support nearby when balance is limited.

**How to perform.** Lift alternate knees with opposite arm swings, placing each foot down under control. Progress from slow marches to brisk marches before a running high-knee version.

**Alignment and range.** Knee height ends before pelvis hiking or leaning backward. A march is a valid low-impact option; do not turn it into jumping just to match a pace label.

**Breathing and pace.** Breathe continuously and reduce the effort before technique breaks down. Pace labels do not change the movement mechanics or establish a suitable intensity for a particular person.

## Stationary bike

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

App labels: `Easy bike`, `Bike, building the pace`, `Bike, sprint finish`, `Steady bike`, `Bike with pushes`, `Bike intervals`, `Easy bike ride`, `steady bike ride`, `Stationary bike`.

**Setup.** Adjust and lock the saddle before mounting. At the bottom of the pedal stroke keep a slight knee bend without rocking the hips; secure the feet as the bike requires.

**How to perform.** Pedal smoothly with knees following the foot direction, shoulders relaxed and a light supported handle grip. Increase resistance or cadence only while the pelvis remains steady.

**Alignment and range.** Avoid an excessively low seat, locked knees, toe-only reaching or bouncing at high cadence. Stop pedalling and dismount deliberately; console and brake procedures vary by bike.

**Breathing and pace.** Breathe continuously and reduce the effort before technique breaks down. Pace labels do not change the movement mechanics or establish a suitable intensity for a particular person.

## Rowing machine

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

App labels: `Rower, building the pace`, `Rower, hard finish`, `Rower with pushes`, `Rower intervals`, `Easy rowing`, `easy rowing`, `Rowing machine`.

**Setup.** Set footrests and straps so the feet are secure, grip lightly with wrists flat and choose manageable resistance before increasing effort.

**How to perform.** At the catch keep arms long and hinge from the hips. Drive legs first, then open the trunk and finish with the arms toward the lower ribs. Recover arms first, hinge forward, then bend the knees.

**Alignment and range.** Do not bend the arms early, overcompress the catch, round the back or lean far backward at the finish. Faster pace should come from a cleaner stronger stroke rather than rushed recovery.

**Breathing and pace.** Breathe continuously and reduce the effort before technique breaks down. Pace labels do not change the movement mechanics or establish a suitable intensity for a particular person.

**Easier version.** Use an easier pace, shorter duration, lower resistance or stable low-impact option while keeping the movement controlled.

## Stair climber

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

App labels: `Stair climber`, `Stair climber, fast finish`, `Stair climber, slow pace`, `slow stair climber`.

**Setup.** Identify the stop control, mount using stable rails and start at a low step rate with the feet securely placed.

**How to perform.** Step one foot at a time with a controlled forward-facing posture and light rail contact for balance. Place enough of the foot on each tread for secure pressure.

**Alignment and range.** Do not support most bodyweight through the rails, skip steps or add backward/sideways patterns in this baseline. Reduce speed before the tread depth becomes difficult to judge.

**Breathing and pace.** Breathe continuously and reduce the effort before technique breaks down. Pace labels do not change the movement mechanics or establish a suitable intensity for a particular person.

## Jump rope

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

App labels: `Jump rope`, `Jump rope intervals`, `Easy jump rope with breaks`, `jump rope intervals (30 s on, 30 s off)`.

**Setup.** Use a rope length and open area that allow a clear arc above and around the body; begin with isolated small jumps or rope-free rhythm practice.

**How to perform.** Keep elbows near the sides and use small wrist-driven circles with low soft jumps, landing under the body with knees unlocked. Reset deliberately after a trip.

**Alignment and range.** The retrieved ACE reference is a brief drill description, so exact rope fitting remains practical-review work. Avoid high tuck jumps or locked-knee landings merely to clear the rope.

## Jumping jacks

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

App labels: `Jumping jacks and marching`, `Jumping jacks and high knees`.

**Setup.** Begin in a clear space with feet together or hip-width and arms relaxed.

**How to perform.** Make a small jump to widen the feet as the arms rise through a comfortable overhead arc, then return softly. For a step jack, move one foot out at a time without jumping.

**Alignment and range.** Keep knees tracking with the feet and reduce arm height if overhead motion pinches. The step version preserves a lower-impact option without forcing a fast rebound.

## Shadow boxing

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

App labels: `Shadow boxing, fast finish`, `Shadow boxing intervals`.

**Setup.** Use clear space and a balanced staggered stance with soft knees, hands near the face and no external hand weights.

**How to perform.** Practise relaxed controlled punches, returning to guard between actions and moving the feet without crossing. Let feet and hips turn together for a rotational punch.

**Alignment and range.** Do not lock the elbows forcefully, swing punches through an exaggerated back twist or sacrifice guard/footwork to match a fast-finish label. This is noncontact practice.

**Breathing and pace.** Breathe continuously and reduce the effort before technique breaks down. Pace labels do not change the movement mechanics or establish a suitable intensity for a particular person.

**Easier version.** Use an easier pace, shorter duration, lower resistance or stable low-impact option while keeping the movement controlled.
