## Renegade Row

Reference relationship: **same-movement**. Status: internal research draft.

**Setup.** Use stable flat-sided dumbbells on a nonslip floor and widen the feet enough for an aligned high plank.

**How to perform.** Shift pressure into the supporting hand, row the other bell toward the ribs, then set it down quietly before switching.

**Alignment and range.** Keep the trunk angle appropriate to the named variation. Let the shoulder blades glide; do not keep them forcibly squeezed for the entire rep.

**Breathing and pace.** Exhale on the pull and inhale during the reach. Pause rather than use torso momentum.

**Common mistakes.** Throwing the chest upward, twisting unintentionally, shrugging to finish or bending the wrists to imitate extra range.

**Easier version.** Reduce load, use chest support or make a suspension row more upright.

**Variation details and limits.** Prevent pelvic rotation and avoid round bells that can roll beneath the supporting wrist.

**When to stop or regress.** End or regress when the described alignment, support or breathing is lost. Stop for sharp pain, pinching, numbness or tingling; individual symptoms need appropriate professional assessment.

**References reviewed.** [PureGym: Renegade Rows](https://www.puregym.com/exercises/back/rows/renegade-row/) ([review limits](sources.md#p79)).

Movement relationship describes reference relevance, not professional validation. Notes are original synthesis; differing equipment/support, progressions, breathing and conservative range choices may be adaptations. See the variant notes and each source's limits.

## Weighted Pull-Up

Reference relationship: **related-only**. Status: internal research draft.

**Setup.** Use a secure dip belt or vest with the load clear of the floor and a step for entry.

**How to perform.** Pull from a controlled comfortable hang until the chin clears the bar without craning, then lower smoothly.

**Alignment and range.** Keep the trunk supported and avoid kicking. Use a comfortable hang and pull height without jutting the chin or forcing the shoulders.

**Breathing and pace.** Exhale during the pull and inhale on lowering. Start the next rep from a controlled position.

**Common mistakes.** Swinging for momentum, neck reaching, uncontrolled drops or grip failure.

**Easier version.** Use assistance or a pulldown; choose a manageable range before adding weight or asymmetric grips.

**Variation details and limits.** Demonstrate controlled bodyweight repetitions before adding load; do not let hanging plates swing during mounting.

**When to stop or regress.** End or regress when the described alignment, support or breathing is lost. Stop for sharp pain, pinching, numbness or tingling; individual symptoms need appropriate professional assessment.

**References reviewed.** [NASM: Pull-Up](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/pull-up) ([review limits](sources.md#npull-up)); [PureGym: Pull Up Exercises](https://www.puregym.com/exercises/back/pull-up/) ([review limits](sources.md#p72)).

<a id="lat-pulldown"></a>
## Lat Pulldown

Reference relationship: **same-movement**. Status: internal research draft.

**Setup.** Secure the thighs under the pad and take an overhand grip somewhat wider than the shoulders.

**How to perform.** Pull the bar in front of the face toward the upper chest, then allow a controlled overhead reach.

**Alignment and range.** Keep a modest stable torso angle; avoid turning the exercise into a large backward lean. Never pull the bar through the head.

**Breathing and pace.** Exhale while pulling and inhale during the upward return. Do not let the stack yank the shoulders.

**Common mistakes.** Excessive lean, behind-neck pulling when not intended, curling only with the wrists or crashing the stack.

**Easier version.** Lower the load and use a comfortable neutral grip or single handle.

**Variation details and limits.** Use the front-pulldown section of the source; its separate behind-neck variation is not the intended catalog movement.

**When to stop or regress.** End or regress when the described alignment, support or breathing is lost. Stop for sharp pain, pinching, numbness or tingling; individual symptoms need appropriate professional assessment.

**References reviewed.** [PureGym: Lat Pulldowns](https://www.puregym.com/exercises/back/lat-exercises/lat-pulldown/) ([review limits](sources.md#p67)).

**Easier version.** Use less resistance, a supported arm position or a neutral grip.

**Variation details and limits.** The angled grip adapts a barbell curl; keep both hands symmetrically placed and avoid using the bar's bends as an excuse for wrist collapse.

**When to stop or regress.** End or regress when the described alignment, support or breathing is lost. Stop for sharp pain, pinching, numbness or tingling; individual symptoms need appropriate professional assessment.

## Assisted Pull-Up Machine

Reference relationship: **same-movement**. Status: internal research draft.

**Setup.** Read the machine's entry sequence and secure knees or feet on the assistance pad while gripping the handles.

**How to perform.** Pull and lower smoothly while remaining centered on the pad, then exit using the machine's steps.

**Alignment and range.** Keep the trunk supported and avoid kicking. Use a comfortable hang and pull height without jutting the chin or forcing the shoulders.

**Breathing and pace.** Exhale during the pull and inhale on lowering. Start the next rep from a controlled position.

**Common mistakes.** Swinging for momentum, neck reaching, uncontrolled drops or grip failure.

**Easier version.** Use assistance or a pulldown; choose a manageable range before adding weight or asymmetric grips.

**Variation details and limits.** On counterweighted models more stack weight gives more assistance. Confirm the specific machine rather than treating its number as lifted weight.

**When to stop or regress.** End or regress when the described alignment, support or breathing is lost. Stop for sharp pain, pinching, numbness or tingling; individual symptoms need appropriate professional assessment.
