# Remaining 107 comparison review

Reviewed November 13, 2026 against development baseline `848cf3e`. This is a second pass over **all 107** comparisons left related after the [first follow-up](research.md).

**28 gained configuration-specific support; 79 remain related.** The full library now has 158 configuration-matched references, 187 earlier same-labelled discovery entries and 79 related comparisons. Configuration support does not mean an exact replication of every internal-note regression or full professional technique approval.

All 107 have an explicit source-specific disposition below. Some outcomes reject a candidate or establish only why an earlier comparison remains inadequate. The [structured audit](research-round2.json) retains the previous selection, exact URLs, consulted video IDs, criteria and differences. The first-pass research record remains historical.

## Review boundaries

Published original descriptions, primary publisher biographies, public provider preview stills and a small number of explicitly recorded visible-player positions were compared. Direct public publisher clips were sampled at recorded times. No login/paywall gate was bypassed. No third-party media or copied page bodies are committed.

The hanging windshield wiper and heel-elevated dumbbell squat are judged against the catalog requirements; earlier internal-note regressions or load choices are separately identified. Some accepted references support only the expressly described phase or configuration. Full continuous motion, narration, every repetition, individual prescription, certification renewal and professional technique approval remain unreviewed.

Publisher identity and credential/context bases are in [source provenance](sources.md). A title or HTTP 200 alone never establishes an exact exercise. The [candidate register](research-candidates.json) includes unselected material without endorsement; the [retrieval ledger](research-pages.json) records access outcomes without redistributing descriptions.
