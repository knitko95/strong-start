## Owner review and grip revision: November 12

The owner marked all six drafts Needs changes. Every item identified poor hand/weight placement; Goblet Squat also had an awkward wrist pose, Floor Press had crossed/intersecting legs, and Single-Arm Bench Press had an awkward free hand. The original exported review is retained locally in `review-submissions/2026-11-12-review.json`; original draft files are retained in `before-grip-revision/`. No visual approval is inferred from automated checks.

Revision 2 replaces the wrist-offset weight placement with a fitted palm grip. A local anatomical frame comes from the middle-finger direction and index-to-pinky span. The handle centre is fitted to the curled finger bones, the thumb is posed around that grip, and the exported weight follows that frame. The kettlebell now has straight gripping horns with a rounded crown, matching both fists. The floor-press knee pole points upward so both knees remain on their own side; the free hand in the single-arm press rests beside the body with a neutral-looking wrist. The lateral raise starts with a small arm clearance from the thighs.

Additional exported-geometry contact checks sample all 121 frames, compare handle position/orientation against the grip, measure finger distances, and check floor-press knee separation. These complement close-up images at both movement extremes; bone-distance checks do not prove the absence of every skin intersection. Previous review notes and Needs changes decisions remain visible until the owner changes them. Review exports identify asset revision 2. No new exercises are attached to the app.

Final revision checks pass for all six clips. Exported dumbbell centres stay within 0.21 mm of the fitted grip centres; floor-press knee centres remain at least 28 cm apart. These are geometry measurements in the authored scale, not human technique thresholds. Inspected 24 close-up views and the corrected floor-press/front and free-hand/side views. Chrome checks pass again at 1280, 390 and 320 px, with no unexpected console errors or external requests. Physical phones and owner acceptance remain outstanding.
