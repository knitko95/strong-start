# Per-exercise audit results

[Scope, outcomes and findings](README.md). All judgments below apply only to the frozen candidate and stated samples. No entry is professionally certified.

<a id="barbell-bench-press"></a>
## Barbell Bench Press

ID: `barbell-bench-press`. **no-additional-discrepancy-observed**. Eligibility: same-movement-written-reference. Animation: barbell-bench-press.

**Reference setup:** Lie with eyes near the racked bar, head/upper back/pelvis supported and feet planted. Set hooks below full arm reach and safeties just below the planned bottom. Wrap thumbs around the bar.

**Reference action:** Unrack over the shoulders, lower toward the mid-to-lower chest with forearms near vertical, then press slightly back toward the rack without contacting it.

**Result:** No additional gross discrepancy identified at the inspected samples. Full movement/skin-contact/professional review remains open.

**Sources:** [1](https://www.puregym.com/exercises/chest/bench-press/barbell-bench-press/), [2](https://www.nasm.org/resource-center/exercise-library/barbell-bench-press), [3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CjHIKDQ4RQo).

**Boundary:** Named exercise reference; compare the linked source setup with the internal technique notes.

## Close-Grip Bench Press

ID: `close-grip-bench-press`. **closer-review-required**. Eligibility: same-movement-written-reference. Animation: close-grip-bench-press.

## Smith Machine Bench Press

ID: `smith-machine-bench-press`. **corrections-required**. Eligibility: source-specific-configuration-evidence. Animation: smith-machine-bench-press.

**Reference setup:** Position a stable bench beneath the fixed bar path; test the unracking rotation and safety stops with an unloaded bar.

**Reference action:** Place the bench so the bottom is over the chest and the forearms stay under the bar; press along the machine's rail without lifting the pelvis.

**Result:** [E01: Smith bar is disconnected from its rails](README.md#e01-smith-bar-is-disconnected-from-its-rails); [E05: Loaded hands do not wrap around the equipment](README.md#e05-loaded-hands-do-not-wrap-around-the-equipment)

**Sources:** [1](https://www.puregym.com/exercises/chest/bench-press/barbell-bench-press/), [2](https://www.muscleandstrength.com/exercises/smith-machine-bench-press.html), [3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z_r6hDOYtO0).

**Boundary:** The Muscle & Strength instructions specify a flat bench under the Smith rails, feet supported and lowering toward the middle chest. Scott Herman's separately credited clip preview shows that rail-guided flat-bench setup. The original article and this video are separate sources, not an embedded-video claim. Rail angle, stops and hook operation remain model-specific.

## TRX Anti-Rotation Press

ID: `trx-anti-rotation-press`. **corrections-required**. Eligibility: same-movement-written-reference. Animation: trx-anti-rotation-press.

**Reference setup:** Use a secure suspension anchor and nonslip floor, following the equipment model's installation instructions. Set the trainer to its approved single-handle mode and test-load before leaning. In the documented two-pass TRX arrangement, place handle A above B, thread B through A's webbing triangle, then A through B's triangle; draw A tight to lock. Check against the manufacturer diagram rather than gripping one unsecured strap. Stand side-on, anchor-side foot behind the other in a close stagger.
