## What changed

- Fitted loaded wrists, palms, planted hands/forearms, bench feet and supine support. Retained legitimate wrist extension for floor supports and the front rack.
- Replaced telescoping/free-floating mechanisms with fixed-length landmines, machine arms, knee levers, hip swing arms and suspension straps. Added actual stations, pulleys, restraints, platforms, cuffs, catches and supports.
- Added distinct hack-squat, glute-drive, glute-ham, neutral pull-up, Smith, leg-press and fan-bike setups. These are mechanically coherent generic illustrations, not exact replicas of every manufacturer's equipment.
- Corrected wrong implements and movement variants: one two-hand swing bell, a barbell rollout, rim-held plates, ball squeeze/single-leg bridges, forefoot/heel supports, ball/TRX foot support, bench/decline setups and a seated medicine-ball twist.
- Reworked step/landing and hand-transfer sequences, alternating rows and knee hugs, pike/dog/inchworm motion, thoracic opening, stretch grasps and medicine-ball release/recovery. Slams use an explicitly retrieved dead ball; wall throws use a rebound illustration.
- Air-bike labels now open a dedicated linked-handle fan-bike demonstration instead of the stationary-bike substitute.

The shared body, 55-bone skeleton, muscle maps and original eighteen accepted scene references are unchanged. Shared helper changes also affect other procedural drafts; this does not add source approval for the 128 excluded reference entries. No catalog names/IDs, account state, saved workout data or persistence behavior were changed.

## Checks and evidence

Its constraints are authoring geometry, not clinical range limits. The [verification record](../../animation-authoring/correction-verification.json) freezes source and manifest hashes and the measured maxima.

Browser checks cover the full 446-clip package in actual WebGL, three poses per clip, the shared character/skeleton, phone and desktop layouts, controls, error/retry and storage/decompression fallback, workout/modal ownership and unchanged synthetic app state. The development build also runs the existing 103 persistence/workout and six offline scenarios. Tests block account/backend mutations.

The final local capture includes 296 entries with six standard views each and 53 entries with additional eight-phase sequences. All 25 standard contact sheets and eight sequence sheets were visually inspected. [Selected before/after images](evidence/corrections/README.md) are retained here. Full images, matrices, contact sheets and a searchable before/after gallery remain in `output/correction-candidate/` inside the audit worktree. The capture tool supports `--output`, `--extra`, `--sequence`, and selective `--only`/`--replace` refreshes.

## Review and publication boundary

Open the [test app](https://dev.strongstart.app/), then **Library > Review all 3D demonstrations**. Pause, scrub and rotate the revised exercises; use Expand for small contact points. The normal exercise/guide flow uses the same assets.

Owner review is still needed for fine finger/skin clearance (especially shoulder/elbow bar support), manufacturer-specific fit and dimensions, tempo/retrieval phases and the intended comfortable range. Source videos were not all continuously replayed. No professional technique/anatomy certification, authenticated-account test or physical-phone test is established. The eight-phase review and numeric constraints reduce specific visible/structural faults; they cannot establish individual suitability.

A concurrent optional Rose athlete character comparison is preserved when integrating current dev. This form review uses the default anatomical instructor; it does not certify skin/equipment fit on the alternate character.

No database migration or production configuration change is pending. This candidate is for dev testing only; production publication requires separate approval of the tested release.
