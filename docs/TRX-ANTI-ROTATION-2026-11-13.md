## Finding and chosen variation

The written setup gap is resolved for the catalog's **chest-level suspension anti-rotation press**. [Tony Gentilcore's original exercise article](https://tonygentilcore.com/2014/07/exercises-you-should-be-doing-trx-anti-rotation-press/) supplies a direct movement reference. The updated [exercise entry](exercise-technique/catalog-core-and-carries.md#trx-anti-rotation-press) uses its inside-foot-back variation and slow chest-height press. A separate manufacturer procedure supplies the equipment configuration. These establish a documented setup, not a clinical consensus or observed performance check.

The reference now covers secure installation, a model-approved single-handle arrangement and test-load, side-on positioning with a controlled whole-body lean, the hand path, trunk control, breathing, regressions and exit. No fixed strap mark or mandatory body angle is needed to define this movement. The selected length must permit its position and tension on the actual equipment; a more upright starting angle reduces demand.
