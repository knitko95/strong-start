Strong Start

Privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Strong Start is a small, personal workout app shared with friends. It builds workout plans and tracks your workouts. This page explains what it saves and who can see it.

What we save

  • Your account: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name on your Google account. If you use a password, it is stored securely by our sign-in provider and we can't see it.
  • What you tell the app in setup: first name, and optionally age, body weight and gender; your goals, where you work out and what equipment you have; your schedule; and anything you mark as painful or limiting.
  • Your workouts: your plan, the weights and reps you log, how sessions felt, and any notes between you and your coach.

If you use the app without an account, everything stays on your device and nothing is sent to us.

Why we save it

Only to run the app: to build and adjust your plan, keep your progress, let you use it on any device, and let your coach help you. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone else.

Who can see it

  • You.
  • Your coach (the person who runs Strong Start), who can see your setup, plan and workout history and send you plan changes.
  • No one else. Other people using the app can't see your information.

Where it's stored

Account and workout data is stored with Supabase on servers in the United States. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Your phone also keeps a copy so the app works without a signal.

Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google,” we receive only your name, email address and Google account ID, which we use to sign you in. We don't access anything else in your Google account.

Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time in Settings. To have your account and everything in it permanently deleted, ask your coach (the person who invited you to Strong Start), and it will be removed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
