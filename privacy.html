Strong Start

Privacy policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Strong Start is a small, personal workout app shared with friends. It builds workout plans and tracks your workouts. This page explains what it saves and who can see it.

What we save

  • Your account: your email address and, if you sign in with Google, the name on your Google account. If you use a password, it is stored securely by our sign-in provider and we can't see it.
  • What you tell the app in setup: first name, and optionally age, body weight and gender; your goals, where you work out and what equipment you have; your schedule; and anything you mark as painful or limiting.
  • Your workouts: your plan, the weights and reps you log, how sessions felt, and any notes between you and your coach.
  • Messages and feedback: coaching conversations include read status shared with the other participant; dismissing a new-message card also marks those messages read. A coach message can include the workout reference shown there. App feedback includes its category, app version/build, screen and device type. Updated app versions send it to our private GitHub tracker with no attached account identity, profile or workout record. Earlier versions use the existing app database and can retain account/workout links until account deletion. Leave out personal, health and login details.
  • Invites and account access: invite names, codes, expiry and use records, and account approval or disabled status.

Existing local-only sessions save plans and workouts in that browser. New accounts require an invite during beta.

Why we save it

Only to run the app: to build and adjust your plan, keep your progress, let you use it on any device, and let your coach help you. We don't use it for advertising or sell it. The service providers described below process data to run the app and manage feedback.

Who can see it

  • You.
  • Authorized coaches, who can see members’ setup, plans and workout history and send plan changes. Coaching conversations are visible to the member and their assigned coach (the coach who invited them). Other coaches cannot read these threads. Plan-update notes from the assigned coach also appear in the conversation.
  • App owners, who can read account profile/access information and manage account disabling or deletion. Authorized people and agents with private-tracker access can review app feedback to improve the app. Coach access is a separate role.
  • Other members do not have access to your information.

Where it's stored

Account and workout data is stored with Supabase on servers in the United States. The live and development apps are hosted on Cloudflare; the earlier GitHub Pages address remains available. The development app uses a separate test database. Test accounts and data do not transfer to the live app.

For reports sent to GitHub, Cloudflare securely queues app reports until GitHub confirms delivery, then removes the queued text/context and keeps a minimal delivery receipt for duplicate protection. If delivery is uncertain, the queued report remains while it is checked. Reports remain in the private GitHub tracker for triage and history; the original database feedback is also retained. Signing in is verified with Supabase; the internal outbox uses an account-derived identifier that is not attached to the GitHub issue. A failed send keeps the unsaved draft only during the current page session.

Your browser keeps a local copy of your plan and workouts. Account changes sync when connected. Open the app while online before using it without a signal; opening it from scratch offline is not guaranteed. Restoring a backup while signed in can replace account data and sync that replacement to other devices.

Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google,” we receive only your name, email address and Google account ID, which we use to sign you in. We don't access anything else in your Google account.

Deleting your data

Check the save status before signing out in Settings. Signing out clears the app’s local saved copy; it does not delete your account. To permanently delete your account, use Settings → Account → Delete my account. Sign in within the last 10 minutes and type DELETE to confirm. You can copy a backup first. Deletion removes your login, plan/settings, workout history, and coach messages, and clears this browser’s saved account copy. Other devices may retain an offline copy until they reconnect. Joining again requires a new beta invite. Paused accounts and accounts awaiting an invite can also delete from their account screen. Coach and app-owner account deletion must be arranged with the app owner.

App feedback is retained after account deletion to help improve Strong Start. New tracker reports have no attached account identity or workout record; older database records lose their profile link and workout reference. The message text and screen/device context remain, and text you entered may still identify you. Contact the app owner to request removal of retained feedback. Deleting an account does not remove backups you copied elsewhere.

Changes
